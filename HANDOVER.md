# Drive Ease: admin guide

This guide covers everyday updates to the Drive Ease website. You don't need any technical knowledge, and nothing you do here can break the website's design.

**Sign in:** go to **your-website-address/admin** and enter the email and password you were given. Change the password the first time you sign in (see *Account* below).

---

## Enquiries: check these daily

Everything sent through the website's enquiry form arrives here, including forms customers send from the Concierge, a car page or their saved cars (the card shows where it came from, the trip type, travellers and bags). WhatsApp messages don't come here; they arrive in your WhatsApp Business app as usual. Opening WhatsApp from the website doesn't create an enquiry here.

1. Open **Enquiries**. The boxes at the top count your enquiries by status. Tap one to show only those. **New** shows enquiries nobody has handled yet.
2. Tap **Reply on WhatsApp** to open a chat with that customer, already greeting them by name and naming the car they asked about. Or tap **Call**.
3. Move the status along as you go: **Contacted** → **Quote sent** → **Confirmed**, or **Closed** when it's done without a booking.
4. **Follow up on:** pick a date to remind yourself. On that day the enquiry appears under **Follow-ups due** (until it's Confirmed or Closed).
5. **Internal notes:** open them on any enquiry to write what was agreed. Only signed-in staff can see notes; customers never do.
6. Use the search box to find an enquiry by name, phone number, email, car or place.

## Insights: which cars people ask about

Open **Insights** to see, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, how many visitors tapped a WhatsApp button for each car, sent a car to family, or sent an enquiry form. Use it to decide which cars to feature, photograph better, or add more of.

- The counts are anonymous. The website never records who tapped, their number or their device.
- A tap means WhatsApp was opened with the message ready. WhatsApp doesn't tell the website whether the customer then pressed send, so you'll usually receive fewer chats than the count.

## Google reviews

1. In your **Google Business Profile**, choose **Ask for reviews** and copy the link (it looks like `https://g.page/r/…`).
2. Paste it into **Site details → Google review link** and save.
3. The website now shows **Review us on Google** buttons. On any enquiry marked **Confirmed** or **Closed**, tap **Ask for a Google review**: WhatsApp opens to that customer with a thank-you message and the link, ready for you to send.

Only ask customers who have actually travelled with you.

## Cars

**Update a car** (for example, new photos):
1. Open **Cars** and tap the car.
2. Change what you need. The bar at the bottom says *Unsaved changes*.
3. Tap **Save changes**. The website updates within about a minute.

**Photos:** tap **Upload photos** or drag photos onto the box. There are three sets, and each gets its own tab on the car's page:
- **Exterior photos.** The first one is the **cover**, shown on the fleet page. Use **Make cover** to change it.
- **Interior & dashboard photos.** Seats, dashboard and cabin.
- **Boot & luggage photos.** The open boot, ideally with a couple of bags in it.
- Only use real photos of your own cars. If a set is empty, the car's page tells customers to ask you for photos.
- Landscape photos taken in daylight look best. The website crops them to a 3:2 shape, so keep the whole car in frame with some space around it.
- Phone photos are fine. They are resized automatically before uploading.

**Prices** aren't shown anywhere on the website. Customers ask about pricing on WhatsApp, where you can quote for their dates.

**Seats and Bags:** keep these accurate. The Concierge never suggests a car with fewer seats or less boot space than the customer needs.

**Hide a car for a while** (in service, sold out for the season): turn **Published** off and save. It disappears from the website but keeps all its details. Turn it back on later. Only use **Delete** for cars you no longer have. Deleting can't be undone.

**Featured** adds a yellow badge and shows the car first on the home page. Feature two or three cars at most.

**Display order:** lower numbers show first on the fleet page.

## Blog

1. Open **Blog** and tap **+ Add post**, or tap an existing post.
2. Add a **Title**, a one-or-two-sentence **Summary** (it's shown on the blog page and in Google results), and a **Cover photo**.
3. Write the article. Use the buttons above the text box: **B** bold, **I** italic, **H** heading, **•** list, **🔗** link. Tap **Preview** to see how it will look.
4. Turn on **Published** when it's ready and tap **Create post** (or **Save changes**).

Drafts stay hidden from the website until **Published** is on.

## Reviews

Add only **genuine** reviews from real customers who have agreed to be quoted. Turn **Published** on to show a review. **Display order** controls which appear first, and the first three also appear on the home page.

> The sample reviews that came with the website are placeholders. Unpublish or delete them before launch.

## Getting found on Google

The website is set up for Google (titles, headings, a page per service, structured data, a sitemap). What moves a local car rental up the results is mostly done outside the website, and only you can do it:

1. **Site details → City** and **Areas you serve.** Every page title and heading uses the city ("Car rental in Pune"), so it must be right. Add the airport, nearby areas and towns you often drive to.
2. **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com): create or claim it with *exactly* the same business name, address and phone number as the website. Pick the category "Car rental agency", add your hours, the website address, and real photos of your cars and office. This is what puts you in the map results.
3. **Google reviews:** ask every happy customer (the **Ask for a Google review** button on confirmed enquiries does it in one tap). Reply to every review.
4. **Search Console** (search.google.com/search-console): add the website, choose *HTML tag*, paste the code into **Site details → Google**, save, then press *Verify*. Then submit `your-website/sitemap.xml` under *Sitemaps*.
5. **Same details everywhere:** list the business on Justdial, Sulekha and similar directories with the same name, address and phone.
6. **Service pages:** under **Site details → Services**, write 150–300 words for each service (what's included, typical trips, areas). Each one is its own page on Google.
7. **Real photos** of your own cars, and a blog post now and then about local trips ("Pune to Lonavala by car"), keep the site fresh and trustworthy.

Rankings take weeks to months to build. Search Console shows which searches you appear for.

## Site details

Phone number, email, address, opening hours, social links, the About page, services and FAQs.

- **WhatsApp number:** digits only, with the country code and no `+` or spaces, e.g. `918879790490`. Every WhatsApp button on the website uses this number.
- **Pre-filled message:** the text typed for visitors when they tap a WhatsApp button.
- **WhatsApp catalogue link:** optional. Your WhatsApp catalogue is managed in the WhatsApp Business app. Updating a car here doesn't change the catalogue, and changing the catalogue doesn't change the website.
- **FAQs:** only include answers you have confirmed, such as deposits, documents and kilometre limits.

Tap **Save changes** at the bottom when you're done.

## Account

**Account → Change password.** Use at least 10 characters. If you forget your password, contact your developer to reset it.

---

### Good to know

- Changes appear on the website within about a minute. If you don't see one, refresh the page.
- The website collects enquiries only. Bookings, payments and availability are handled by you, on WhatsApp or by phone. The Concierge only suggests cars from what you've entered; it never promises availability.
- After 10 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.
- If you try to leave a page with unsaved changes, you'll be asked to confirm first.
